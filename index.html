SwiftIQ EduFlow

A smart school management solution designed to simplify school administration, improve organisation, and support better decision-making. One School. One Flow.

What it covers

  • Student admissions and records
  • Fees management: invoices, payments and statements
  • Timetables and attendance
  • Exams, assessments and academic reports
  • Classes, streams, subjects and curricula (CBC/CBE)
  • Communication, discipline, activities, staff and documents
  • Works on phones, tablets and laptops

Software productPoint of Sale System

SwiftIQ POS

A practical point-of-sale solution designed to simplify product sales, transaction recording, checkout workflows, and receipt generation.

What it covers

  • Product search and barcode scanning
  • Shopping cart and checkout
  • Cash, M-Pesa and card payment selection
  • Receipts and sales records
  • Inventory, purchases and returns
  • Customers, expenses and employees
  • Reports, daily summaries and cashier shifts
  • Works across every branch
